Student Help Portal

Answers to the questions we hear most, from registration to research placements. Can't find what you need? Reach out to the department office.

Registration & Enrollment

  • Log into the student portal and go to Registration > Add/Drop. Changes are open without approval during the first two weeks of the term. After that, you'll need a signed override form from your advisor, available at the department office.

  • Minors can be declared any time before the start of your final semester. We recommend declaring by the end of your sophomore year so prerequisite courses fit your schedule.

  • Official transcripts are requested through the Registrar's online portal, not the department. Electronic copies typically arrive within 24 hours; mailed copies take 5–7 business days.

Courses & Coursework

  • Sometimes, with instructor permission. Email the instructor directly with a short note on relevant experience or coursework, and CC your advisor. Approval is granted case by case, not guaranteed.

  • Many instructors post past materials on the course page. For a broader archive, the CS Student Association maintains a shared drive — ask any TA for the current link.

Research & Careers

  • Browse faculty research pages for areas that interest you, then email 2–3 professors with a specific question about their work, not a general "can I join your lab." Office hours are also a good low-pressure way to introduce yourself.

  • The department doesn't place students directly, but our Careers Office hosts recruiting events each fall and spring, and the alumni mentorship list is open to any declared major.
